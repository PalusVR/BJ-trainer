Model

Infinite-deck EVs, computed exactly — not a transcription of the images. Double on any two cards, double after split, no resplits, ties push, blackjack pays 3:2 (never a decision, so it's dealt out).

Full read: dealer's exact two-card hand is known. Partial read: only the upcard is known plus a read that the hole card is a spot 4–10. Against an ace the dealer has already peeked, so the hole card is 4–9 there.
