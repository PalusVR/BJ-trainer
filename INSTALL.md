# Blackjack Trainer — install guide

**Link:** https://palusvr.github.io/BJ-trainer/

It's a web page that installs like an app. No store, no account. Works offline after the first load.

**iPhone / iPad** — open the link in **Safari** → tap **Share** (square with the up arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

**Android** — open the link in **Chrome** → **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.

**Computer** — open in Chrome or Edge → click the **install icon** in the address bar (or ⋮ → Install). Or just bookmark it.

**Keep a copy** — it's a single file. Download `index.html` from the repo and open it in any browser; it runs with no internet.

**Your data** — stats and bankroll save in your own browser, on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

**First two minutes** — Drill deals immediately; tap your play. The dropdown switches between reading both dealer cards and the pip read. Set your game's rules on the Rules tab and the charts recompute. Tap any Chart cell to see the EVs. Play is a real shoe with chips.
