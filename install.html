Blackjack Trainer

Get it on your phone

It's a web page that installs like an app. No store, no account, nothing to sign up for. Works offline once it has loaded once.

Open the trainer

iPhone · iPad

  1. Open the link in Safari (it has to be Safari for this to work).
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with the arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

It now opens full-screen from your home screen like any app, and keeps working with no signal.

Android

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then confirm.

Computer

  1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the small install icon at the right end of the address bar, or ⋮ → Install Blackjack Trainer.

Or just bookmark it. Any browser works.

Keep your own copy

The whole trainer is a single file. Download index.html from the project and open it in any browser — it runs from your desktop with no internet at all.

Your data

Stats and the play-table bankroll are saved in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Each device keeps its own record, and clearing your browser data clears it.

First two minutes

  1. Drill deals immediately. Tap your play. It pauses on a miss so you can see the cost; Setup → Pace changes that.
  2. The dropdown top-right switches between reading both dealer cards and the pip read.
  3. Rules — set the game you actually play. The charts recompute as you change them.
  4. Chart — tap any cell to see every option's EV and the dealer's bust odds.
  5. Play — a real shoe with chips. Coaching flags any play that departs from the chart.
